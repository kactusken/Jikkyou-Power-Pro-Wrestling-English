# Screenshots

## Main Menu

![The main menu, with Versus selected and its description below](screenshots/release-1-main-menu.png)

The mode buttons and the screen title are redrawn in English, and the line at the bottom describes each mode.

![The Versus setup screen: rules, players, time limit and the two wrestlers](screenshots/release-2-setup.png)

Menus, rule names and the wrestlers' names are in English throughout the setup screens.

## Match

![The start of a match, with the commentator's credit at the bottom](screenshots/release-3-match.png)

The opening caption naming the commentator is translated.

## Winner Interview

![A post-match interview: the winner's portrait, time, finishing move and his answer](screenshots/release-4-interview.png)

![The Max Voltage entry form with an English letter grid for the wrestler's name](screenshots/release-5-entry-form.png)

The kana name grid is replaced with an English letter grid, so career wrestlers get Western names.

## Career Hub

![The Max Voltage hub: month, ratings, and the Profile, Record, Magazine, Transfer, Moves, Costume and Diet menu](screenshots/release-6-hub.png)

The hub's menu and labels are redrawn in English.

## Story

![The opening of the Max Voltage story: the new wrestler joins Super Japan Pro-Wrestling](screenshots/release-7-story.png)

The whole Max Voltage story is translated, with the promotions and wrestlers given English names.

## Show Card

![A show card at the Tokyo Big Dome with five bouts and a result](screenshots/release-8-card.png)

The card title, bout names and result lines are in English, with the shortened roster names that fit the screen.
