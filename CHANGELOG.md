# Changelog

## v1.0
First release.
- The whole game in English: menus, rules and help, the 50-wrestler roster, 185 move names, the
  post-match interviews, and the Max Voltage career mode with its story, events and endings.
- Screen titles and menu graphics redrawn in English; an English letter grid for name entry.
- Two bugs in the original game's Max Voltage story fixed: the faction choice in May 1999 is
  now recorded, and SJPW's "faction war's on hold" reaction to Max Mercury now shows
  ([docs/MAX_VOLTAGE.md](docs/MAX_VOLTAGE.md#bugs-fixed)).
- A player's guide, [docs/guide/](docs/guide/index.html): the gameplay FAQ merged with the Max
  Voltage documentation and story map, with names matching the translation and a Secrets section.
